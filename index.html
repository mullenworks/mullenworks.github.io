About me

I’ve worked on products at Google, Kin, and JPMorgan Chase, helping teams simplify complicated experiences, decide what matters most, and make ideas easier to use and understand.

When founders come to me, I first work out where the confusion sits. If people understand the product but the pitch still falls flat, I look at the story. If the value is hard to explain or the demo feels scattered, I look at the product itself. From there, we can decide what needs to happen next.

FAQs

Who do you typically work with?

Early-stage founders and small product teams with a real product or prototype, whose story or demo isn’t landing yet with investors, customers, or their own team.

How do we decide between a pitch strategy and a prototype?

If the story is unclear, start with Pitch & Narrative Strategy. If the story is clear but the product is hard to show, start with the Prototype for Pitch Sprint. Often the story comes first, because it decides what is worth prototyping.

What happens during the 20-minute chat?

We look at what you’re building, where the story or demo feels fuzzy, and whether a sprint makes sense. A messy draft is fine; you don’t need to prepare a deck. If there’s a fit, I follow up with a recommended scope and price.

How much time will I need to put in?

Beyond the chat, expect to share existing drafts or product access up front and react to work at agreed check-ins. Exact time depends on the scope we settle on together.

What if we need revisions or our direction shifts mid-sprint?

Each sprint includes a structured polish pass so we can refine the story or flow together. If new scope, extra product journeys, or a major pivot comes up mid-sprint, we’ll pause, agree on the adjusted timeline and fee in writing, and keep moving with no surprises.

What will I receive at the end?

A pitch sprint ends with a 10–12 slide narrative deck. A prototype sprint ends with an interactive prototype and demo framing notes. A broader engagement ends with a diagnostic, a prioritized roadmap, and a next-step plan.

How do billing and timelines work?

Focused sprints typically take 1–2 weeks once we have your existing materials. Sprints are billed 50% upfront to reserve your spot on the calendar and 50% upon final handoff.

How do you handle confidential product ideas or pre-launch decks?

Everything you share before and during an engagement is treated as confidential. I don’t share your materials, decks, or product details publicly, and I’m happy to sign a standard mutual NDA before kickoff. Once paid in full, you own all final deliverables created for your project.

What if MULLEN isn’t the right fit?

I’ll tell you in the chat, and point you to a better option if I know one. There’s no obligation to continue after the call.
